About This Tradition

A heritage carried through generations

Bihula Bishari is part of a rich tradition of folk belief, storytelling, devotion and cultural memory associated with Bihar and the wider eastern Indian cultural landscape.

The tradition brings together the legendary story of Bihula, devotion to Bishari and the cultural practices through which communities preserve their beliefs and memories.

Over generations, these traditions have been expressed through worship, oral storytelling, artistic practices, local customs and community celebrations.
