Welcome

A story carried through generations

Bihula Bishari is one of the enduring folk traditions associated with the ancient cultural landscape of Anga. At its heart is the legendary story of Bihula, whose devotion, determination and courage have become part of the region's collective memory.

The tradition of Goddess Bishari continues to connect faith, folklore, art and community life, preserving a distinctive part of Bihar's living cultural heritage.
