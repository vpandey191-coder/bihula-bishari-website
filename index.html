About the Tradition

A story carried through generations

Bihula Bishari represents a rich strand of folk culture associated with Bihar and the wider eastern Indian cultural landscape.

At its heart are devotion, courage, faith and the enduring relationship between communities and their sacred traditions.

This website is dedicated to documenting and presenting that heritage in a respectful and accessible way for visitors in Bihar, India and around the world.

Sacred Tradition

Goddess Bishari

Bishari is revered in folk religious traditions associated with protection, devotion and the relationship between people and the sacred.

The traditions surrounding Bishari are preserved through worship, storytelling, local customs, artistic expression and community memory.
