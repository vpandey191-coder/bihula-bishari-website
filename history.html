A Living Tradition

History preserved through memory

Bihula Bishari belongs to the rich tradition of regional folklore and folk religious culture associated with Bihar and eastern India.

Much of this heritage has been preserved through oral storytelling, devotional practices, local customs, artistic traditions and community memory.

Rather than existing only as a written historical record, the tradition continues to live through people who remember, celebrate and pass its stories to the next generation.
