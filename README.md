## About the Project

A cultural heritage website documenting the centuries-old Bihula Bishari tradition of Anga Pradesh, including the Bihula Bishari Geet, the Bihula-Bishari legend, Goddess Bishari, and the traditional Manjusha art associated with the festival its rich body of folklore, devotional traditions, poetry, art and community heritage.

At the heart of the tradition is the centuries-old story of Bihula and Lakhendra, preserved and performed through the traditional **Bihula Bishari Geet**, a Hindi/Angika folk-poetic tradition associated with the Bihula Bishari festival.

The Bihula Bishari tradition is also closely associated with the famous **Manjusha art** of the Anga region. Manjusha is a distinctive traditional art form whose imagery and visual storytelling are deeply connected with the Bihula-Bishari legend and its devotional traditions.

The festival, its songs, storytelling traditions and Manjusha art together form an important part of the cultural heritage of the Anga region.

This website aims to document and present this heritage in an accessible form for visitors in India and around the world.

# Bihula Bishari

A cultural heritage website dedicated to **Bihula Bishari**, **Goddess Bishari (Manasa)**, and the rich folklore, devotional traditions and cultural heritage associated with **Anga Pradesh**.

## About the Project

Bihula Bishari is a cultural documentation website created to present the stories, traditions, beliefs and heritage surrounding the Bihula Bishari tradition in an accessible form for visitors in India and around the world.

The website brings together information about:

- Bihula Bishari
- Goddess Bishari / Manasa
- Bihula and Lakhendra
- Chandradhar Saudagar
- Traditional folklore
- Devotional traditions
- Cultural heritage of Anga Pradesh
- Historical and community traditions
- Cultural photographs and visual documentation
- Related cultural videos

## Website Sections

The current website includes:

- **Home** — Introduction to Bihula Bishari and the cultural tradition
- **About** — Background, cultural significance and purpose of the website
- **History** — Historical and traditional background of Bihula Bishari

Additional cultural sections and media resources can be added as the website develops.
